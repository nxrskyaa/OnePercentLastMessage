# X launch post

🔋 1% — LAST MESSAGE
My entry for the @DlicomApp AI Game Jam.

I built this around a familiar problem: your phone is at 1%, and there's still one message you need to send.

So in this game, you become the encrypted message itself. Fly through a 3D signal harbor, squeeze through moving gates, dodge trackers and reach the receiver before the battery dies.

Sometimes you're carrying a deploy key. Sometimes it's one last gm. Sometimes Mom just wants to know where you are.

Nitro buys you speed but spends extra battery. Trackers take privacy. Power pickups give you another chance to get the reply through.

✦ On your flight

• Three channels: Tidal Conduit, Prism Archive and Solar Relay
• Three courier craft: Skimmer, Needle and Comet
• Curved skyways, climbs, dives, moving gates and rotors
• DILI guidance, scan pulses and nitro
• Keyboard and touch controls; English and Indonesian
• Downloadable result cards with your profile and flight stats
• An original soundtrack and synthesized SFX

Built with Codex for the Dlicom AI Game Jam. Free to play in your browser.

🎮 Play: https://last-message-lac.vercel.app/

Get it delivered, then drop your result card below. I'm curious how much battery you can bring home.
