# Flight guide

You are the final encrypted message. Reach the bright receiver before the battery reaches zero. Privacy affects your score; losing privacy alone does not end the run.

## Your first flight

Choose a channel, press **Transmit**, read the message and launch. The skyway follows its own bends. Move the craft inside that corridor instead of trying to steer the entire route. Follow the pale cyan line and aim for the **◇** marker.

| Key                         | Action                                              |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| A / D or Left / Right       | Move across the corridor                            |
| Q / E                       | Climb / dive                                        |
| W or Up                     | Accelerate                                          |
| S or Down                   | Brake; overrides nitro and relay bursts             |
| Shift                       | Nitro; spends extra battery                         |
| Space                       | Scan nearby hazards and nodes; five-second cooldown |
| Escape                      | Pause / resume                                      |
| Click-drag inside the world | Optional mouse steering                             |

The HUD keeps a PC key diagram visible. Direction cues show which key moves toward the next opening or power node. **AVOID RED** points around a tracker. When the cue says **HOLD YOUR LINE**, release the steering/climb keys.

On phones and touchscreen tablets, drag the left analog stick to steer and climb/dive. Small movements make fine adjustments; release to stop steering. Hold Thrust, Brake or Nitro with your other thumb. Tap Scan or Pause.

## What to look for

- **Cyan boosters:** restore 0.07% battery, up to the starting 1.00%.
- **Blue relays:** clean center passes award points and a short speed burst.
- **Red trackers:** avoid them; contact takes battery and privacy.
- **Mechanical apertures:** move to the bright opening. Brake early if you need time to line up.
- **Rotors:** cross through a gap between blades. The marker follows an open quadrant.
- **Public relays:** speed at a privacy cost. Safe relays preserve privacy.
- **Tips:** collectibles add score, with a chain bonus for quick successive pickups.

Cruise speed can finish a run when you collect enough power nodes. Nitro is a choice, not a requirement. Missing a few pickups or clipping a gate is recoverable; holding Boost through the whole course wastes power.

## Channels and replay

Tidal Conduit is the first channel to learn. Prism Archive changes the vertical sequence; Solar Relay mirrors lateral approaches and increases obstacle pressure. Each course is 1.8 km with Departure, Sky Locks, Engine Room and Last Approach sectors.

Retry starts another flight immediately. Results show the outcome, time, battery, privacy and score. Download Card exports a PNG with your local name, X handle and profile image where supported. Share copies a result summary without login.

## Comfort and performance

Settings include quality, music/SFX volume, mute, reduced motion and camera feedback options. Start with Auto on desktop and use Low on devices with slow frames. Pausing or hiding the tab holds the flight and stops audio. Best scores and settings are saved in the browser; clearing site storage resets them.
